# Branding

**System → Branding.** Colours, logo, favicon and fonts.

You can keep as many saved themes as you like; exactly one is **active** at a time. That makes
seasonal or campaign looks safe to prepare in advance and switch in one click.

## Themes

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| **Set active** | Makes this theme live |
| **Duplicate** | Copies it — the safe way to try a change |
| **Delete** | Removes it. Not available on the active theme. |

**The safe way to experiment:** duplicate the active theme, edit the copy, activate it. If you
don't like it, reactivate the original. Nothing is lost.

## Colours

**Primary** is the main colour — buttons, links, highlights. **Primary (hover)** should be a shade
darker. **Accent** is used sparingly for credits and emphasis.

Under **Advanced palette** are the full light-to-dark scales. You rarely need these; the three
above cover the visible surface. If you do change them, keep them in order — light at 50, dark at
900 — or shadows and hover states stop making sense.

## Logo and favicon

Both are **web addresses**, not uploads. Host the image somewhere and paste the link.

- **Logo** — leave blank to use the built-in mark. A wide transparent PNG or SVG works best.
- **Favicon** — the little icon in the browser tab. 32×32 or larger, square.

## Fonts

Separate choices for **headings** and **body**. Options: Inter (default), System UI, Georgia,
Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono.

A common, effective pairing is a characterful heading font with a plain body font — Space Grotesk
headings, Inter body. Setting both to something decorative makes long text tiring to read.

## Custom CSS

Injected into every page when the theme is active. Powerful and easy to break things with — there
is no preview and no validation. Change one thing at a time and check the site after each.
