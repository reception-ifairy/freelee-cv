# Publishing tab

Whether a persona is visible, and how changes to it are tracked.

| Switch | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Published** | Off = invisible to everyone, editable by you. The safe way to work on something live. |
| **Featured on home** | Shows in the *Featured personas* block on the front page. |
| **Premium** | Marks it as premium in the catalogue. |
| **Position** | Sort order. Lower numbers first. |
| **Credits per message** | A surcharge on top of normal token cost. Usually 0. |

## Version pinning

Off by default, and off is right for most personas.

**Off** — you edit, you save, everyone gets the new version on their next message. Simple.

**On** — the persona gets a proper draft/publish cycle. Edits go to a draft nobody sees until you
press Publish, each published version is frozen permanently, and you can roll back.

Turn it on when a persona is doing something where a surprise change is a real problem: anything
regulated, anything being sold, anything where you'd need to prove what it said last March.

Leave it off when you just want to fix a typo without ceremony.

## A safe way to change a live persona

1. Untick **Published**
2. Make and test your changes
3. Tick **Published** again

Visitors see the old version until step 3, and nobody watches a persona change under them
mid-conversation.
